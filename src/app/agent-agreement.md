## Background

A. Campus&Me operates a student-focused accommodation marketplace in Nigeria. Campus&Me may receive accommodation information from students, landlords, caretakers, agents and other sources, verify properties and units, publish verified listings, receive accommodation requests from students, coordinate viewings and transactions, and track transactions, commissions, referrals and settlements.

B. The Agent is an independent, third-party service provider who wishes to receive qualified accommodation Leads from Campus&Me and to help students find and secure accommodation.

C. The parties wish to record the terms on which Leads are assigned, transactions are tracked, and commission is earned and paid.

D. Payment are handled off-platform, though records are provided digitally on the agent dashboard

## 4. Lead Assignment

4.1 **Lead reference.** Each Lead has a unique reference (for example, **CH-4821**) that Campus&Me records against your account.

4.2 **Information provided.** Campus&Me may provide Lead information such as the Lead ID, preferred location, budget, room or unit type, expected move-in date, accommodation preferences and other requirements. Campus&Me may withhold the student's name and contact details until you accept the Lead.

4.3 **When a Lead is assigned.** A Lead is assigned to you when Campus&Me sends it to you through the platform (or another channel Campus&Me notifies you of) and records the assignment.

4.4 **Accepting or rejecting.** You may accept or reject a Lead through the platform. Acceptance is complete when you confirm it on the platform. You should reject a Lead promptly if you cannot serve it, and you may be asked to give a short reason.

4.5 **Response time.** You must accept or reject each Lead within **[RESPONSE TIME]** of assignment, and after acceptance must make first contact with the student within **[FIRST CONTACT TIME]**.

4.6 **No response.** If you do not respond within the response time, Campus&Me may treat the Lead as rejected, withdraw it, and reassign it to another Agent or handle it itself. Repeated non-response may lead to reduced Lead allocation under clause 17.

4.7 **Reassignment.** Campus&Me may also reassign or withdraw a Lead if: you reject it; you fail to progress it within a reasonable time; the student asks for a different Agent or to stop; Campus&Me reasonably suspects a breach of this Agreement; or the Lead is no longer valid. Reassignment before you have accepted a Lead gives rise to no commission claim. If Campus&Me reassigns a Lead you had accepted and worked on, any commission claim is determined under clause 10.

4.8 **Introduction.** After you accept a Lead, Campus&Me may facilitate the introduction between you and the student and may share the student's contact details with you for the sole purpose of serving that Lead.

4.9 **Attribution record.** Campus&Me records each assignment, acceptance, rejection, reassignment and the time of each, and these records are the primary evidence of attribution (subject to clause 10.8).

4.10 **No ownership of the student relationship.** Being assigned a Lead does not make you the owner of the student relationship. The student remains a Campus&Me-Referred Lead for the purposes described in clause 10.

## 8. Commission and Service Fee

8.1 **Accommodation Service Fee.** The current commercial model is an Accommodation Service Fee of **15%** of the Rent for a completed Transaction. This is an **illustrative commercial structure** and is not necessarily the final rate (see clause 8.6). **[LEGAL REVIEW: who is charged the service fee and how it is lawfully disclosed and collected.]**

8.2 **Standard allocation.** Unless Campus&Me notifies otherwise for a Transaction, the Service Fee is allocated as follows:

| Share | Recipient |
|---|---|
| 5% of Rent | Campus&Me |
| 5% of Rent | The Agent assigned to, or participating in, the Transaction ("Agent Commission") |
| 5% of Rent | The Referrer |

8.3 **Illustration.** If the annual Rent is ₦500,000 and the Service Fee is 15%, the Service Fee is ₦75,000, allocated ₦25,000 to Campus&Me, ₦25,000 to the Agent and ₦25,000 to the Referrer. See **Schedule A**.

8.4 **Not every Transaction has three parties.** A Transaction may have no Referrer, or no Agent, or more than one Agent or Referrer. Campus&Me may set a different allocation for a Transaction in those cases and will apply it consistently and record it. Where a share has no recipient (for example, no Referrer), it is retained by Campus&Me unless Campus&Me decides otherwise and records that decision.

8.5 **Referrer.** The Referrer may be a student who submitted the property, another approved Agent, or another eligible source recognised by Campus&Me. Campus&Me decides who qualifies as the Referrer under its published referral rules and attribution policy.

8.6 **Changes.** Campus&Me may change the Service Fee or allocation for future Transactions by giving you at least **[COMMISSION CHANGE NOTICE PERIOD]** written notice (including through the platform or by email). A change does not affect Transactions already completed or Leads you have already accepted before the change takes effect, unless you agree otherwise.

8.7 **Calculation basis.** The Service Fee and Agent Commission are calculated on the Rent actually agreed for the completed Transaction, as confirmed by Campus&Me. Unless Campus&Me states otherwise in writing, caution fees, legal fees, agreement fees, service charges and utility payments are not part of the Rent for commission purposes. **[LEGAL REVIEW]**

8.8 **Rounding.** Campus&Me applies one consistent rounding rule to monetary calculations, and records the Rent and rate used for each Transaction.

## 9. Commission Eligibility and Lifecycle

9.1 **When commission becomes payable.** Agent Commission becomes payable only when **all** of the following are true:

(a) the Lead is properly attributed to you under clause 10;

(b) the accommodation Transaction is genuinely completed;

(c) you have submitted the transaction information Campus&Me reasonably requires;

(d) Campus&Me has verified the Transaction where it needs to;

(e) there is no unresolved fraud allegation, dispute, cancellation or material breach connected to the Transaction or to you; and

(f) any required payment settlement for the Transaction has occurred.

9.2 **What does not earn commission.** You do not earn commission merely because you receive a Lead, contact a student, schedule a viewing, or show a property.

9.3 **Lifecycle.** Commission moves through these stages, as shown on the platform and described in **Schedule B**:

**Lead Assigned → Accepted → Contacted → Viewing → Transaction → Commission Eligible → Approved → Paid**

9.4 **Commission payment period.** Approved commission will be paid within **[COMMISSION PAYMENT PERIOD]** after the conditions in clause 9.1 are met and the commission is approved, through the payout route in clause 13.

9.5 **Cancellations and refunds.** If a Transaction is cancelled, reversed or refunded, in whole or in part, the related commission is cancelled or reduced to match, under the cancellation and refund terms shown to the student for that Transaction **[CANCELLATION AND REFUND POLICY]**. If commission was already paid, Campus&Me may recover the corresponding amount to the extent permitted by law, including by set-off against future commission after notice to you. **[LEGAL REVIEW: set-off and recovery rights.]**

9.6 **Withholding.** Campus&Me may withhold commission that is genuinely disputed or under investigation, but only for as long as reasonably needed to resolve it under clauses 17 and 18, and will tell you why.

## 10. Lead Attribution and Non-Circumvention

10.1 **Principle.** A student introduced to you through Campus&Me remains a **Campus&Me-Referred Lead** for attribution, commission, reporting and non-circumvention purposes, for the Attribution Period.

10.2 **What this does not mean.** This clause is about **commercial attribution and non-circumvention**. It does not prohibit you and a student from speaking directly, from exchanging contact details, or from communicating outside the platform, and it does not restrict the student's freedom to choose where to live or whom to work with. The student remains free to deal with anyone.

10.3 **What you must not do.** You must not deliberately bypass Campus&Me to avoid its Service Fee, referral obligations, transaction records or other platform arrangements. In particular, you must not:

(a) complete, or help complete, a Transaction with a Campus&Me-Referred Lead without recording it on Campus&Me;

(b) classify a Campus&Me-Referred Transaction as an unrelated private transaction when it is reasonably connected to the Campus&Me introduction;

(c) introduce the student, or a property owner, to another person so that person completes the Transaction to avoid Campus&Me;

(d) introduce a property owner directly to the student for the purpose of avoiding the Service Fee; or

(e) conceal a Transaction, understate Rent, or split or restructure payments to reduce the fee.

10.4 **Attribution Period.** The Attribution Period for a Campus&Me-Referred Lead is **[ATTRIBUTION PERIOD]**, starting on the date of the initial introduction recorded by Campus&Me. **[LEGAL REVIEW: reasonableness and enforceability of the period and of this clause.]**

10.5 **Transactions within the period.** If, within the Attribution Period, a Campus&Me-Referred Lead rents accommodation in a Transaction that is reasonably connected to the Campus&Me introduction (including where the property is different from the one first discussed, or where the student returns to you directly), then:

(a) the Transaction must be recorded on Campus&Me within **[TRANSACTION REPORTING PERIOD]**;

(b) the Service Fee, allocation and commission rules in clauses 8 and 9 apply; and

(c) if you recorded it accurately and promptly and you are the attributed Agent, you remain eligible for Agent Commission.

10.6 **Transactions after the period.** A Transaction that occurs after the Attribution Period ends is not covered by this clause, unless it is a renewal, extension or direct continuation of a Transaction that began within the period, or Campus&Me can show that the delay was intended to avoid the Service Fee.

10.7 **Multiple possible sources.** If a Campus&Me-Referred Lead could be attributed to more than one Agent, Referrer or source, Campus&Me decides attribution using the process in clause 10.8.

10.8 **Fair attribution process.** For any attribution dispute, including where:

(a) two Agents claim the same Lead;

(b) a student says they had already contacted an Agent before the Campus&Me assignment;

(c) two students submitted the same property;

(d) a property was already in an Agent's portfolio; or

(e) an Agent says a Transaction was unrelated to Campus&Me,

Campus&Me will: ask each affected party for their account and supporting evidence (such as messages, call logs, listing records, timestamps and the Campus&Me assignment history); review the evidence fairly and without assuming that its own records are automatically correct; give written reasons for its decision; and allow you to ask for a review under clause 18. Where evidence is unclear, Campus&Me may split the allocation fairly between the parties concerned, but it will not allocate more in total than the Service Fee structure allows unless a written exception is recorded.

10.9 **Duplicate submissions.** A duplicate submission of a property is not automatically a duplicate property. Campus&Me may record multiple submissions for the same property or unit, and decides Referrer attribution based on which submission first provided enough verifiable information to identify the property or unit, adjusted where a later submission provided materially more useful information.

10.10 **Survival.** This clause continues after termination for Campus&Me-Referred Leads whose Attribution Period had started before termination, as described in clause 20.
