## Background

A. Campus&Me operates a student-focused accommodation marketplace in Nigeria. Campus&Me is an intermediary and coordinating platform: it collects and verifies accommodation, approves and publishes listings, receives viewing requests, coordinates viewings, facilitates the rental transaction, and tracks and distributes the applicable amounts after payment.

B. The Agent is an independent accommodation provider who wishes to submit available accommodation to Campus&Me and take part in viewings and rentals that come through the platform.

C. Campus&Me does not assign students to Agents. Instead, Agents contribute verified accommodation supply, and Campus&Me contacts the Agent associated with a listing when a student requests a viewing.

D. The parties wish to record the terms on which accommodation is submitted, viewings are coordinated, and Agent Commission is earned and paid.

## 4. Submitting Accommodation

4.1 How the model works. Campus&Me does not assign students to you. You submit your available accommodation to Campus&Me. If Campus&Me verifies and approves it, it becomes available on the marketplace. When a student requests a viewing of an approved listing, Campus&Me contacts the Agent associated with that listing. Your relationship with Campus&Me is therefore connected to the accommodation you provide, not to students assigned to you.

4.2 Submission is not a listing. A submission is a potential listing, not automatically a listing. Campus&Me controls what appears on the marketplace. You may not publish a listing yourself, and submission does not guarantee approval or publication.

4.3 Process. Each submission goes through the following steps: Campus&Me receives it; checks for duplicates; contacts, visits or verifies the accommodation; inspects it where needed; and approves or rejects it. Approved accommodation becomes visible to students.

4.4 What you must provide. For each submission you must give accurate information, including the Property and Unit, location, room type, the rent set by the landlord, any additional charges, facilities, availability date, photographs or media you are entitled to share, and the landlord, caretaker or authorised representative details Campus&Me reasonably requires.

4.5 True rent. The rent you submit must be the rent actually required by the landlord. You must not inflate, pad or restructure the rent, or add charges not authorised by the landlord or by Campus&Me, in order to capture additional money.

4.6 Authority. You confirm that you have the authority of the landlord or owner (or their authorised representative) to market the accommodation through Campus&Me, and you will give reasonable evidence of that authority if asked.

4.7 Keeping it current. You must tell Campus&Me promptly if a Unit becomes unavailable or rented, or if its price, charges or other material terms change. Campus&Me may remove, suspend or mark as expired any listing whose availability it cannot confirm.

4.8 Rejection and withdrawal. Campus&Me may reject a submission, request corrections, or withdraw an approved listing for reasons such as inaccuracy, duplication, unavailability, failed verification, unresolved complaints or suspected breach of this Agreement. Campus&Me will give you a reason where reasonably possible.

4.9 Multiple Agents. Campus&Me works with multiple external Agents and does not give any Agent exclusivity or an automatic right to every accommodation or opportunity in an area.

## 6. Viewings and Communication

6.1 Campus&Me is the intermediary. Students request or book viewings through Campus&Me. Campus&Me receives the request, contacts the Agent associated with the listing, and coordinates the viewing. Campus&Me remains the intermediary through the eventual rental.

6.2 Your participation. When Campus&Me contacts you about a viewing, you must respond within 2hrs, confirm whether the Unit is still available, and take part in arranging and attending the physical viewing as Campus&Me coordinates. If you do not respond in time, Campus&Me may treat the Unit as unconfirmed, pause or mark the listing unavailable, and arrange the viewing through another route.

6.3 Communication through Campus&Me. Campus&Me coordinates communication between the student, you, the landlord or property owner, and Campus&Me personnel. As part of the normal platform workflow, you do not receive the student's direct contact information, and you should not independently communicate with the student outside the coordination Campus&Me provides.

6.4 Contact details. You must not ask a student for personal contact details for the purpose of dealing outside Campus&Me. If a student volunteers contact details in the ordinary course (for example, at a viewing), you may speak with them in the ordinary course of accommodation dealings, but clause 13 still applies and you must not use that contact to bypass Campus&Me.

6.5 Campus&Me platform agents. Campus&Me has its own platform agents, who may handle student communication, viewing coordination, physical viewing participation, inspection, verification and transaction coordination. The platform agent is not necessarily the same person as you, and you have no claim to the platform's own share by reason of their involvement.

## 8. Accommodation Service Fee and Commission

8.1 Service Fee on top of rent. The current commercial model adds a 15% Accommodation Service Fee on top of the advertised rent. For example, if the rent is ₦500,000, the Service Fee is ₦75,000 and the total paid by the student is ₦575,000 (before the landlord's caution fee and other legitimate charges). This is the current intended model and may change under clause 8.9.

8.2 Standard allocation. Unless Campus&Me notifies otherwise for a Transaction, the Service Fee is allocated as follows:

| Share | Recipient |
|---|---|
| 5% of Rent | Campus&Me platform |
| 5% of Rent | External Agent associated with the accommodation ("Agent Commission") |
| 5% of Rent | Property Referrer |

8.3 Illustration. For a ₦500,000 rental, the rent of ₦500,000 goes to the landlord, ₦25,000 to the External Agent, ₦25,000 to the Property Referrer, and ₦25,000 to Campus&Me. See Schedule A.

8.4 Based on roles. The 5% shares are based on the roles involved in the Transaction. The roles of External Agent and Property Referrer can overlap: if you are both the External Agent and the Property Referrer for a Unit, you are entitled to each share for which you qualify, subject to this Agreement.

8.5 No external Agent or Referrer. Not every Transaction has all three parties. Campus&Me may discover and source accommodation itself, so there may be no External Agent or no separate Property Referrer. Where a share would otherwise belong to an absent party, Campus&Me retains that share. Campus&Me supports flexible allocation and will record the allocation it applies to each Transaction.

8.6 Your compensation is the 5% share only. Your compensation as External Agent under this Agreement is the 5% Agent Commission. You may not charge the student or landlord a separate agent fee for a Campus&Me Transaction unless Campus&Me has expressly introduced and authorised one in writing.

8.7 Other charges. The Service Fee is separate from the landlord's caution or security deposit (which belongs to the landlord), and from other legitimate accommodation charges. The caution deposit and other landlord charges do not form part of the Service Fee.

8.8 Calculation basis and rounding. The Service Fee and shares are calculated on the rent actually agreed for the completed Transaction, as confirmed by Campus&Me. Campus&Me applies one consistent rounding rule and records the rent and rates used for each Transaction. [LEGAL REVIEW]

8.9 Changes. Campus&Me may change the Service Fee or allocation for future Transactions by giving you at least 2 weeks written notice (including through the platform or by email). A change does not apply to Transactions whose payment has already been made, unless you agree otherwise.

## 9. When Commission Is Earned

9.1 Earned only on a completed Transaction. Agent Commission is earned only when the accommodation Transaction is completed, meaning the student has decided to rent and the required rent plus the 15% Service Fee has been paid through the Campus&Me process, and Campus&Me has confirmed the Transaction.

9.2 What does not earn commission. You do not earn Agent Commission merely because you submitted a property, the property was approved, a student viewed the property, or a viewing was completed. A viewing does not automatically create a commission entitlement. (Your share of the inspection fee under clause 7 is a separate matter.)

9.3 Conditions. In addition to clause 9.1, commission is payable only if:

(a) you are the External Agent associated with the accommodation under this Agreement;

(b) you have complied with this Agreement in relation to the Transaction, including accurate submission and transaction information;

(c) there is no unresolved fraud allegation, dispute or material breach connected to the Transaction or to you; and

(d) the Transaction has not been cancelled or reversed.

9.4 Lifecycle. The stages are shown in Schedule B: Submitted, Verified and Approved, Viewing Requested, Viewing Coordinated, Inspection Fee Paid, Viewed, Rent and Service Fee Paid, Transaction Confirmed, Commission Distributed.

9.5 Distribution timing. After the Transaction is confirmed, Campus&Me distributes the applicable amounts. Your Agent Commission will be paid within a maximum of 48 hours after Transaction confirmation, through the payout route in clause 12.

9.6 Cancellations and refunds. If a Transaction is cancelled, reversed or refunded in whole or in part, the related commission is cancelled or reduced to match, under the cancellation and refund terms that apply to the student for that Transaction [CANCELLATION AND REFUND POLICY]. ## 10. Referral Attribution and Duplicate Submissions

10.1 Referral is tied to the property. The Property Referrer reward is for referring or submitting accommodation, not for referring tenants. It is tied to the Property or Unit, not to the student who rents it. There is no reward under this model for one student referring another student.

10.2 Who can be a Referrer. A Property Referrer may be a student, an external Agent, or another eligible person recognised by Campus&Me. If you submit accommodation that Campus&Me did not previously have, you can become the Property Referrer for it, and if you are also the associated External Agent, clause 8.4 applies.

10.3 First valid submission. The first valid submission of a particular Property or Unit receives the referral attribution. For example, if Agent A submits Room 12 first and Agent B later submits the same Room 12, Agent A is the original referral source. Campus&Me preserves this attribution, and it survives beyond the original listing submission so a Transaction can be traced back to its source.

10.4 Duplicates. Campus&Me does not publish multiple identical listings for the same Unit. Duplicate detection is based on the actual Property or Unit, not just names, and may use the property or lodge name, building, address or location, room or unit, room type, landlord or caretaker, price, photographs and other identifying characteristics. A duplicate submission triggers review rather than a second public listing.

10.5 A "valid" submission. A submission is valid if it gives enough accurate information to identify the Property or Unit and is not fake, fraudulent or made without authority. Campus&Me may review edge cases (for example, where a later submission provided materially more useful information) and decides attribution fairly, based on documented evidence.

10.6 Fair process. For any attribution dispute, Campus&Me will ask each affected party for their account and supporting evidence (such as messages, listing records and timestamps), review it without assuming its own records are automatically correct, give written reasons for its decision, and allow you to ask for a review under clause 19. Total allocations will not exceed the Service Fee structure unless Campus&Me records a written exception.

10.7 Records. Campus&Me records who submitted the accommodation first, when, which Property or Unit it relates to, whether it was approved, whether it produced a Transaction, and who is entitled to the referral share.

## 13. Non-Circumvention

13.1 Why this exists. Campus&Me introduces and coordinates the parties and provides marketplace, verification, coordination and transaction infrastructure. It is not merely generating leads for Agents. This clause protects against transactions being deliberately moved outside Campus&Me to avoid its fees.

13.2 What this clause is not. This clause does not prevent legitimate communication in the ordinary course of accommodation dealings, and it does not restrict a student's freedom to choose where to live. Its objective is to prevent deliberate circumvention of Campus&Me's commercial relationship and fees.

13.3 What you must not do. Where Campus&Me has coordinated a viewing for you, or you have otherwise participated in a Campus&Me introduction, you must not deliberately:

(a) complete, or help complete, a rental with the student directly, outside the Campus&Me transaction process, to avoid the Service Fee or Campus&Me's records;

(b) conceal a completed rental that originated through Campus&Me, or describe it as unrelated when it is reasonably connected to the Campus&Me viewing;

(c) understate the rent, split or restructure payments, or add or remove charges to reduce the Service Fee;

(d) introduce the student or landlord to another person so that person completes the rental to avoid Campus&Me;

(e) otherwise act with the aim of avoiding Campus&Me's transaction structure; or

(f) fail to tell Campus&Me promptly if a student you met through a Campus&Me viewing approaches you about renting that Unit or another of your Units.

13.4 Protected period. Clause 13.3 applies for 90 days after the Campus&Me-coordinated viewing, including for other Units you control that the same student is shown or asks about. 

13.5 Reporting. If a rental occurs within the protected period involving a student whom Campus&Me introduced to you, you must record it with Campus&Me within 48 hours so the Campus&Me process can be applied.

13.6 Disputes. If there is a dispute about whether a rental is connected to a Campus&Me introduction, clauses 10.6 and 19 apply.

13.7 Continuation. This clause continues after termination for viewings that took place before termination, for the protected period.
